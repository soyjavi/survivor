# Storm Safety

Storms bring strong wind, heavy rain, falling trees and flying debris. Secure what you can before the storm arrives, and stay inside when it hits. Much of the damage in a storm comes from objects that were left loose outside, so the preparation matters as much as the shelter.

## Why it matters

Wind speed rises quickly, and objects that weigh several kilograms can become projectiles within seconds. Most storm injuries happen to people who went outside to check on something, or who were standing near glass when it broke. A house prepared in daylight, with loose items stored and an interior room chosen, is much safer than one organised in a rush with the wind already pulling at the doors.

## Before

- Bring loose objects inside: furniture, bins, tools and plant pots.
- Secure roof coverings and check that gutters and drains are clear.
- Fill containers with water and charge phones, torches and radios.
- Move vehicles away from trees and tall structures.

Check the forecast and the official warnings for your area, and act on them early rather than waiting for the first gusts.

## During

1. Stay indoors, away from windows and external walls.
2. Go to an interior room, or a cellar if there is one, and keep the door closed.
3. Keep torches and radios within reach, in case the power fails.
4. Stay away from damaged areas of the building, and from the roof.

Close internal doors to limit the spread of any damage. Draw curtains or blinds so that flying glass does not reach the room.

## If caught outside

- Find a low, sheltered place away from trees, poles and open ground.
- Do not shelter under isolated trees, because they can fall or drop branches.
- Cover your head, and crouch low, keeping away from metal fences and poles.

## After

- Check for injuries and damage, and give first aid where needed.
- Avoid fallen power lines and damaged trees. Treat every line as live.
- Listen to the radio for updates before moving, and wait for the all-clear.

Take photographs of damage for records before you clear anything, and do not climb onto a damaged roof to inspect it.

## Practice before you need it

1. Walk the house each season and list every loose item in the garden.
2. Test the torches and radio, and check that spare batteries are stored in the interior room.
3. Show each adult how to switch off the power at the main switch, without standing in water.
4. Agree the household signal for an all-clear, so that people do not leave the room too early.

## Adjusting for conditions

In a flood, do not walk through moving water, because it can knock a person over quickly and hide hazards. Move to the upper floor only if the water is rising and you have no other option. In a snowstorm, keep the power source and the heating away from the windows, and ventilate carefully to avoid carbon monoxide build-up.

## Securing the outside

Start with the items that move most easily, such as trampolines, garden furniture and gazebos, and anchor or store them first. Check fences and gates, and tie down any sheds or greenhouses with straps fixed to solid ground. Trim overhanging branches that are already weak or dead, because they are the most likely to fall. Bring in the wheelie bins, lock the garage and make sure the drains are clear so that water does not pool against the walls. Working in daylight, before the wind rises, is far safer than doing this during the storm.

## Common mistakes

- Opening a window to relieve pressure, which lets wind into the room.
- Leaving bins, furniture or tools in the garden.
- Driving through floodwater or under trees weakened by wind.
- Going out to look at the damage while the storm is still active.

## Checklist

- Loose objects are inside or secured.
- Water, torches, radios and phones are ready.
- The interior room is chosen and checked.
- Vehicles are parked away from trees.

## When it fails

If the roof or a window fails, move the household to the interior room, close the door and cover the gap with a blanket or board if it is safe to do so. If the house becomes unsafe, leave by the planned route and go to the meeting point.

**Warning:** a storm can return from another direction. Stay inside until the all-clear is given.
